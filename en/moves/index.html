PokéSurvivor moves and their evolutions

There are 274 moves across all 18 types. Your Pokémon attacks on its own with the active one, and when you level up you pick cards to learn new moves or upgrade them up to level 5. 254 of them can evolve: max them out, get the upgrade card they ask for (Power, Vigor, Reflexes…) and a golden evolution card will appear.
