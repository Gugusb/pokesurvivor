PokéSurvivor special items

Chests and bosses drop special items that stack for the entire adventure. Rifts offer a choice of three, sometimes with exclusive rewards. Super Potion has its own active slot: choose it before departure and use it with 0.

  • LeftoversContinuously restores a little HP.
  • Choice BandRaises damage, but prevents switching moves.
  • Quick ClawShortens move cooldowns.
  • Focus SashSurvives one fatal hit. Consumed when triggered.
  • Shell BellDealing damage restores a little HP.
  • Amulet CoinEarn more coins at the end of the adventure.
  • Lucky EggRaises experience gained.
  • Life OrbRaises damage at the cost of a little HP when attacking.
  • Choice ScarfRaises movement speed.
  • Rocky HelmetHurts attackers that make contact.
  • Assault VestReduces incoming damage.
  • Move LinkerGrants one chance to link moves.
  • Lucky MedalAdds one choice when leveling up.
  • Candy JarStores extra PP above the normal maximum; this surplus recovers more slowly.
  • Courage MedalMelee casts may restore PP to every move in their slot.
  • FlamethrowerRanged hits may burn enemies.
  • Bug NetSlightly improves the chance of catching Pokémon.
